# Privacy Policy — NetScope

Last updated: November 13, 2026

NetScope is an app for monitoring network devices (MikroTik, Ubiquiti and Mimosa) from your phone. We are Team NetScope; this policy explains what data the app handles.

## Data the app stores
- **The devices you add**: name, IP address, port, username and password, and the site (point) the device belongs to.
- This data is stored **only on your phone**; passwords are encrypted with the system's secure storage.
- **We do not send this data to us or to anyone else.** There is no account and no NetScope server.

## Connections the app makes
- **Your devices**: the app connects directly to the devices you add, on your network, to read their status and perform the actions you request (reboot, disconnect a client, enable/disable a port).
- **Device image library**: the app reads device pictures from a public GitHub repository and caches them on the phone.
- **Ping page**: the app measures response time to public sites (Google, Cloudflare, YouTube, WhatsApp, Facebook) and loads their logos from Google's public favicon service.
- These services see your connection's IP address like any normal internet request; no data about you or your devices is sent with these requests.

## What the app does not do
- No ads, no analytics or tracking.
- It does not collect location, contacts, photos or any personal data.
- It does not sell or share any data.

## Deleting data
Delete a device inside the app, or uninstall the app, and all of its data is removed from your phone.

## Children
The app is intended for network technicians and operators, not for children.
